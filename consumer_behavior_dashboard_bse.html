Key findings

What the data says

Illustrative scenario

The ad-engagement gap is the strongest pattern here. Consumers in the top ad-engagement band bought at roughly half the rate of the bottom band. It is a weak correlation (r = −0.12), so treat it as a question to test, not a proven effect.

Engagement level is not a buying signal

Buy rates are 21–23% at high, medium and low engagement.

Purchase intent is a coin flip

Purchase likelihood is about 50% overall and does not move with engagement.

No strong relationships in the data

All pairs of measures have |r| ≤ 0.12. Likes, shares, comments, clicks and time on platform move independently.
